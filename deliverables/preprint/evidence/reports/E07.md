## Assessment and limits

Recorded changes support at least one destination rotation through mutable configuration. They do not establish client refresh frequency, cache behavior, failure handling or universal use of the same resource. Destination blocking can interrupt connections even when a resolver exists; conversely, a cached destination may remain usable after resolver access is blocked. The original assertion that domain takedowns cannot interrupt the operation is too broad.

Monitoring scripts and historical output document an observation method, not a monitoring service verified to be running today. No historical indicator in this report is presented as a current blocklist.
