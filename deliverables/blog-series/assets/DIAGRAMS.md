# Article diagrams

Twelve source-derived diagrams, provided as editable SVG and 1800-pixel PNG.
The adjacent diagrams.json maps each figure to its article, evidence IDs and limits.
Evidence IDs refer to the linked preprint evidence PDFs.

Rebuild from the series directory with `python build_diagrams.py`.
Requires Python, Pillow, PyMuPDF and fontconfig with a sans-serif font.

Arrows describe the labeled code flow or relationship, not observed victim execution.
Amber annotations identify conditions and evidentiary limits. Dashed boundaries
separate recovered material from unobserved behavior. The screen-block example
is schematic, and the repository Venn diagram is not proportional to counts.
