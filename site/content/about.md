Ilja Krasnjanski is an independent security researcher publishing as KrasForge. His work on UNK_DeadDrop examines recruitment lures aimed at developers, malware collection routines and the infrastructure supporting them.

This investigation began with three phishing messages he received in June and July 2026. He traced the associated artifacts through static analysis, repository history and configuration records, and developed the companion YARA rules and `unk-scanner` to help other analysts inspect related files. The series and its papers document the methods, findings and gaps in the available evidence.

## Site analytics

This site counts page views with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an open-source analytics service. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. For each view it records the page, the referring site, your browser, operating system, screen width and language, and an approximate country derived from your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored. To recognise repeat visits, GoatCounter keeps your IP address and browser identifier in memory for at most eight hours and never writes them to its database. The counter script loads from `gc.zgo.at`. Details are in [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).
