## Exhibits

All exhibits were produced by reading bytes only. The two helper scripts (`tools/pngchunks.py`,
`tools/c2pa_static.py`, kept beside this file) walk PNG chunk headers and JUMBF boxes, decode
CBOR with a small built-in decoder, and parse certificate DER with the `cryptography` library.
They perform no signature verification, no network access and no image decoding. The image was
not uploaded anywhere. Provider manifest identifiers, instance IDs and the leaf-certificate
serial are redacted, consistent with E14's statement that they are omitted.

Extraction: `python3 -I tools/pngchunks.py Post.png`

```text

103464    IDAT  3595006
3698482   IEND  0
iend_end_offset=3698494 trailing_bytes_after_IEND=0
```

The `caBX` chunk (the C2PA chunk type for PNG) sits directly after IHDR and holds 103,419
bytes; IEND ends exactly at end-of-file, so zero bytes follow it. There is no tEXt/iTXt/XMP chunk.

### Exhibit E14-6. The "validated" result is a record embedded at signing, not a fresh check

### Claims without a retained primary artifact

- The statement that "the signature validated" (S1, `openssl x509` "verified"): no verifier
  output, OCSP response or trust-list check is retained in the workspace; the only validation
  data is the signer-embedded record in Exhibit E14-6.
- The visual description of the poster (malformed title kerning, cast match) and the claim that
  the thumbnail shows "the same poster": only byte identity of the thumbnail and matching aspect
  ratio were re-derived here; no visual comparison was recorded as an artifact.
