## Account-status limitations

The record discusses an account returning 404 while its name remained unavailable for registration. That pattern supported a suspension hypothesis, with acknowledged alternatives. It is not a provider confirmation and does not establish the current account state. Nearby numeric account identifiers were also examined without a discriminating sibling-account pattern. Registration adjacency alone is not evidence of coordinated account creation.

## Assessment

The strongest evidence is the preserved revision sequence and repeated staging structure. Claims about who controlled an account, why it became unavailable, or what the unrecovered payload would do require additional evidence. E14 separately covers the poster's recorded provenance metadata.
