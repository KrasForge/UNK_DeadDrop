Evidence reports E01 to E21

These are edited analytical reports prepared from retained case records. They contain selected artifact excerpts and identified secondary records, with source fingerprints, extraction methods, redactions and limitations. They are not complete forensic exports. Historical findings retain their observation dates.

The public reports omit raw contact rows, usable credentials, personal identity leads and raw malware samples. Their findings do not establish execution, victim impact or attribution beyond the stated evidence.
